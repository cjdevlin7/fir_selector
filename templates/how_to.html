How to use FIR Selector

Pick a color, select FIRs, and export a full-resolution PNG.

  1. 1. Pick a color

    Choose a swatch from the palette, or use the custom color picker. Whichever is highlighted as the "active color" gets applied to the next FIR you select.

  2. 2. Select FIRs

    Click directly on a FIR's outline on the map, or search by name or ICAO id in the sidebar list and click a result — either way selects it in the active color and adds it to the list. Clicking an already-selected FIR again removes it.

  3. 3. Adjust as you go

    Change the active color any time before selecting more FIRs. Use "Select filtered" to select every FIR currently matching your search, the × next to an entry to remove just that one, or "Clear all" to start over.

  4. 4. Set a background

    Pick Dark, Light, or a custom color. It updates both the live map preview and the exported image.

  5. 5. Export

    Click "Export PNG" to download a full-resolution image of your current selection, styled to match the background you picked. Each export gets a unique, timestamped filename.
